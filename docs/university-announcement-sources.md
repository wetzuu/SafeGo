# University announcement sources

SafeGo uses university administration as the authority for class suspensions and delivery-mode changes. A central or supreme student government may repeat or clarify an announcement when the institution itself recognizes that body, but the administration wins if two posts conflict.

Facebook pages are not treated as a dependable unauthenticated live feed. A post becomes an active SafeGo alert only after its publication time, affected campus or level, class mode, and validity period are known. A page link alone is never interpreted as “classes suspended.”

## Reviewed post examples

The three Facebook photo URLs supplied during research are retained in `lib/safego/university-sources.ts` as reviewed evidence examples for Mapúa University, José Rizal University, and PUP Sta. Mesa. They are deliberately not seeded as current alerts because Facebook did not expose enough public metadata to independently confirm their post time and current validity.

## Acceptance checklist

1. The page belongs to the university, a first-party office, or a central/supreme student government named by the institution.
2. The post clearly names the affected campus, education level, and class mode.
3. The post has a publication time and a specific effective date or period.
4. Later corrections or resumptions replace the earlier alert.
5. An LGU-wide suspension is shown separately and does not get attributed to a university.
